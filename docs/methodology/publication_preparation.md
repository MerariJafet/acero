# Methodology — Publication Preparation

ACERO never publishes. It PREPARES a dossier for human scientific review and, once a human
approves, exports it LOCALLY only.

## Export preconditions (all required)
1. Publication policy: human-reviewed, automatic publication off.
2. Readiness == READY_FOR_HUMAN_SCIENTIFIC_REVIEW (the Sprint-11 ladder ceiling).
3. Comprehension demonstrated (the Sprint-9 comprehension gate).
4. Gate status complete (the Sprint-10/11 inline gate).
5. No unresolved contradictions.
6. An approved review by a HUMAN (not ACERO) that BINDS to the exact dossier content — a
   dossier changed after approval blocks the export.
7. A central claim and stated limitations are present.

## Anti-rubber-stamp
An approval requires the reviewer to acknowledge every review-critical section, demonstrate
comprehension, and state a reason. The approval carries a content hash so it cannot be reused
for a modified dossier.

## What an export is NOT
Not a publication, not a discovery, not an experimental validation. The AI-use declaration in
every export states that ACERO is not an author and claims no discovery.
