# Analogy Validation

An analogy is valid only if it survives, in order: (1) structural — same governing
form and (near-)complete role mapping; (2) dimensional — a shared dimensionless
group is truly dimensionless in BOTH systems using their own (different) units;
(3) mathematical — role isomorphism; (4) limits — shared conservation in the limit;
(5) predictive transfer — a transferred prediction (resonance) is VERIFIED by
simulating both systems in the sandbox; (6) counterexample — surface/geometric
similarity without deep structure is flagged MISLEADING; (7) skeptic. Surface
similarity is weighted 0.05 in the deep score. Even valid analogies must state their
regime limits (where they break). Rejected/misleading analogies are preserved as
evidence of what does not work.
