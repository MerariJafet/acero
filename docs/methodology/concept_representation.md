# Concept Representation

A concept is defined by MULTIPLE structured definitions, not one LLM paragraph:
lexical, operational, mathematical, causal, behavioral, and by-constraints. Example
(Temperature): lexical="thermal measure"; operational="result of a measurement
procedure"; mathematical="<KE> ~ (3/2) kT"; applicability="local equilibrium";
failure regime="strongly out of equilibrium". Each concept also carries units,
dimensions, variables, assumptions, invariants, symmetries, and applicability
regimes. Meaning is queryable (see `concept_engine.md`), and every concept from an
LLM is unverified until checked.
