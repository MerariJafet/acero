# Methodology — Cross-Domain Transfer

Shared mathematical structures (diffusion, saturation, oscillation, conservation, growth,
competition, networks, transition, noise, equilibrium, instability) recur across sciences —
but a shared FORM does not imply a shared MECHANISM, variables, or units.

The Multi-Domain benchmark transfers **saturation** from chemistry (Michaelis–Menten) to
genetics (Hill regulation): the structure is shared, the mechanism and variables are not,
so no identity is claimed. The Analogy Engine validates each transfer (structural /
dimensional / predictive) and records the domain, scale, regime, and validity limits — the
World Model never treats cross-domain evidence as independent replication of one phenomenon.
